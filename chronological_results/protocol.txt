Exploratory chronological refitting and five-seed sensitivity protocol
Fixed before constructing new queries or computing new model scores.

Scope: sensitivity of the previously selected BiLSTM versus PersonalRecency comparison and fixed equal-weight top-ten reciprocal-rank fusion. This is not a renewed six-model architecture selection. Include refitted popularity and quarter-transition controls as descriptive references. Do not tune fusion weights or select an architecture on these outcomes.

Calendar design: fitting targets and histories end no later than Q4 2014. Tuning targets occur in 2015; take the first retained quarter in that year with at least one preceding retained quarter. Test targets occur in 2016 or later; take the first retained quarter in that interval with at least one preceding retained quarter. Include all prior retained quarters in each history. No later quarter enters its query. These are frozen-model predictions at successive historical dates, not simultaneous forecasts from 2015 for all later years.

People: retain the existing disjoint 30,000 fitting and 3,000 tuning identifiers from the JobHop training release. Use the existing confirmation and fusion-validation identifiers as two distinct evaluation samples. Each evaluation history contributes one query. Fitting includes every eligible pre-cutoff prefix and rebalances weights so each contributing history has total weight one. Do not replace excluded people to achieve a desired sample size. Retain the existing date-resolution, unknown-label and catalogue conventions; report their retrospective-curation limitations.

All learned count statistics and neural weights are refitted using pre-2015 fitting queries. Frozen catalogue and text embeddings are reused as fixed measurement assets, not represented as historically available in 2014. Hyperparameters and architecture derive from the earlier study; this experiment is exploratory rather than an untouched prospective replication. The two chronological samples reuse people from previously inspected evaluation files. No claim of globally leakage-free historical deployment is made.

BiLSTM: same 384 input, 16 hidden units per direction, 3,007 outputs, dropout 0.1, RMSprop learning rate 0.001, batch 128, gradient clipping 1, resume-balanced uniform target loss. Seeds 42,43,44,45,46, all reported. Early stopping after at least ten epochs and five nonimproving tuning epochs; maximum 45. At cap, report whether final-five-epoch best improvement is below 0.002. Select only the highest tuning NDCG checkpoint per seed. No extension or seed exclusion based on evaluation scores.

GPU budget: one non-resetting 1,800-second phase including benchmark, five fits and scoring; 4.5 GiB allocated-memory limit on local RTX 2060. Benchmark on fitting queries only; discard benchmark weights. Admit if projected minimum ten epochs for all seeds plus inference is below 80% of the cap. Fit seeds round-robin to avoid giving one seed an unbounded budget. If the cap is reached, save partial records and report incompleteness; do not silently reset or extend it.

Evaluation: full 3,007-candidate rankings, k=10, fixed two-digit ISCO broader targets absent from all history. Fixed RRF neural and recency weights 0.5, constant 60, input depth ten. Report all seeds, mean and sample standard deviation across seeds, and range of primary contrasts. Primary contrasts: neural minus recency NDCG overall and unseen-positive, their cohort shift; fusion minus recency and neural NDCG; fusion minus recency repeated recall; fusion minus neural broader recall. Seed-averaged history bootstrap intervals: 5,000 paired-history draws with maximum absolute centred deviation across these seven contrasts, separate family per evaluation sample. Also resample seeds and histories jointly to describe training-plus-history uncertainty, with explicit limitations of only five training seeds. Thresholds remain cohort shift 0.01, aggregate superiority 0.005, recall retention -0.01.

Verification before scoring: strict calendar bounds; history before target; disjoint fitting/tuning/evaluation IDs; count-statistic provenance and weight totals; no original fitted checkpoint or count model loaded. Independent CPU metrics and fusion-rank reconstruction after scoring. Save hashes, timings, hyperparameters and complete training records. Keep user-level arrays and raw data local; distribute only aggregate records and analysis code.
