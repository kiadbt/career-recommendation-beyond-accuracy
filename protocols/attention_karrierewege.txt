This is a post hoc architectural sensitivity analysis, specified after the
BiLSTM/recency/fusion KARRIEREWEGE results were inspected. It is not a fresh
confirmatory dataset or an independent preregistration.

Use the existing QuarterSelfAttention checkpoints (seeds 42 and 43), unchanged,
on the exact 56,536 KARRIEREWEGE queries already constructed. No optimisation,
hyperparameter search, checkpoint reselection, or attention-based fusion.
Retain the fixed complete 3,007-occupation catalogue and stable tie breaking.
Report NDCG@10 for all histories and unseen-positive histories, and Recall@10.
Average the two seed metrics within each history before population aggregation.
Contrast attention minus personal recency overall, within unseen-positive
histories, and the difference between those gaps (cohort shift).
Repeat on the previously fixed 43,663 unique nonmatching sequences.
For each population, use 5,000 paired-history bootstrap draws, seed 20261018
for all histories and 20261019 for the sensitivity. A common 95th percentile
of maximum absolute centered deviations gives simultaneous intervals across
the three contrasts within that population. These are not joint intervals
across architectures, datasets, or populations. Report seed means descriptively.

Hash protocol, scripts, queries and checkpoints before prediction. GPU cap
180 seconds, allocation cap 4.5 GiB, batch 128, two CPU threads. Fail closed
without restarting if the budget is exceeded. Verify scalar metrics for every
history, and padding and batch-size invariance on a fixed sample. Preserve
unfavourable or inconclusive outcomes. No GPU training or new data downloads.
