Additional self-attention baseline — 14 October 2026

Post hoc extension after prior test findings; not an untouched confirmatory test.
Implement a quarter-set self-attention baseline, not a claimed reproduction of
SASRec. Same frozen 384-dimensional occupation embeddings, historical quarter
sets, 3007 candidates, 30000 fitting resumes, 116387 fitting prefixes and 3000
tuning resumes as existing models. No new features or labels.

One causal Transformer encoder block, projected width 64, two attention heads,
feed-forward width 256, learned positions up to 256 quarters, dropout 0.1,
pre-layer normalisation and final layer normalisation. The last valid quarter
state feeds a linear 3007-class head. Unused padded positions are masked.
AdamW lr 0.001, weight decay 0.01; batch 128, gradient clipping 1; existing
resume-balanced multi-target loss; float32; seeds 42 and 43.

Checkpoint selection only on full-target tuning NDCG@10. Minimum 10, maximum
45 epochs per seed; stop after five consecutive nonimproving tuning epochs.
At the cap, require best-score improvement over the preceding five epochs
below 0.002, otherwise report under-convergence and do not claim adequacy.
No hyperparameter sweep or test-driven retraining. Single fixed architecture.
Benchmark fitting data before admitting the two fits. GPU wall-time cap 900s,
4.5-GiB allocation ceiling, local RTX 2060; preserve resumable states.

After both fits meet stopping criteria, freeze checkpoint hashes and evaluate
on original JobHop test and Decorte external test, with unchanged weights.
Do not replace the originally selected BiLSTM or its confirmatory comparisons.
Report NDCG@10 and Recall@10 overall and full-target NDCG among unseen-positive
resumes. Three contrasts versus the saved recency baseline: overall gap,
unseen-positive gap, and their difference. 5000 paired-resume bootstrap draws,
seed 20261015, max-absolute centred radius across these three per dataset.
Also report the paired overall NDCG difference versus the existing BiLSTM
descriptively. No new fusion selection for this model.
